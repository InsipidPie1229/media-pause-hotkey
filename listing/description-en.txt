Media Shortcut

Control web media in the tab you most recently played, even while another app is focused.

• Pause or resume with a global shortcut (default Ctrl+Shift+1, or Command+Shift+1 on Mac). You can change it in Chrome's shortcut settings.

• Change playback speed (0.25×–4×) with shortcuts or popup presets.

• Optional extension volume (experimental): 0–500% applied separately to the site's own volume, without moving the site's volume slider.

• Works with standard video and audio on HTTP and HTTPS pages. No data is sent to any server.
